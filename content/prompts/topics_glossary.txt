## Medical claims — sourced or cut

- Allowed bodies ONLY: **ACOG · WHO · RCOG · NICE · NHS · Egyptian Ministry of Health.**
- Every medical claim needs a real URL you actually consulted, listed in the `sources` field.
  The URL will be opened and checked. A dead link or one that does not say what is claimed
  fails the whole article.
- 🔴 If you cannot source a claim, DROP THE CLAIM. Do not soften it, do not hedge it, do not
  attribute it vaguely to "studies" or "doctors". "I don't know" is a correct answer here.
- The source decides the FACT. The user decides the WORD — never ship a literal translation of
  an English medical term because a source used it.
- Prefer MedlinePlus, NHS, WHO ELENA — they fetch cleanly. ACOG returns HTTP 402 and CDC returns
  403 to automated fetch; if a claim can only be sourced there, either find the same fact at a
  fetchable body or state plainly that the claim was dropped for lack of a verifiable source.
